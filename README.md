# 🛡️ NiveshShield-AI

### AI-Assisted Investment Awareness and Risk Insights

NiveshShield-AI is a project focused on using Artificial Intelligence to help users understand investment-related information and become more aware of potential financial risks.

The goal is to make financial information easier to explore while promoting transparency, informed decisions, and responsible use of AI.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore how AI can support investment awareness.
- Help users understand potential investment-related risks.
- Present financial information in a clear and understandable way.
- Encourage informed decisions rather than relying blindly on automated outputs.
- Promote responsible and transparent use of AI in financial technology.

## ✨ Key Areas

## 🔐 Responsible Use

NiveshShield-AI is intended to support investment awareness and exploration of AI-assisted financial technology.

- AI-generated outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate.
- Investment decisions should not rely solely on AI-generated information.
- Do not expose API keys, passwords, account details, or private financial information.
- Do not interpret unvalidated outputs as guaranteed returns or risk-free investment advice.
